How Coffee Shop Began

Coffee Shop started in 2020 with one simple goal: serve honest, delicious coffee in a place that feels like home. What began as a tiny counter has grown into a neighborhood favorite, but our values haven't changed.

We work directly with small coffee farms, roast our beans in small batches, and bake our pastries fresh every morning. Every cup is made with care by people who genuinely love what they do.

What We Stand For
